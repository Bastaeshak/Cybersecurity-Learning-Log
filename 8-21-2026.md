## Key Takeaways

* SOC investigations require understanding **why** something is suspicious, not just recognizing an alert.
* The Cyber Kill Chain helps explain the progression of an attack, while MITRE ATT&CK provides more detailed descriptions of attacker behavior.
* Alert investigation requires gathering evidence before deciding on a verdict.
