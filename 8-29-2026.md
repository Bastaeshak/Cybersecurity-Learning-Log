### What I Learned

- Severity should be based on actual impact, account/data sensitivity, targeting, and evidence - not the alert's reported severity.
- Legitimate authentication does not automatically mean the activity performed afterward was authorized.
- Verdict, severity, escalation, and threat-intelligence notification should be evaluated separately.
- Continued improving my ability to defend SOC verdicts using evidence instead of assumptions.
